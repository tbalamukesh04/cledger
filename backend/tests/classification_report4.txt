  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Pattern: amount_detected=True  currency_detected=True  verb=False  negative=False
  Typical score: 4

  No verb is required and no negative guard fires because these messages contain
  no NEGATIVE_PHRASES and no question mark.

  Examples:

    "1000 ZMW"                               — same
    "estimate is around 2000 ZMW"            — cost estimate, not a payment
    "budget for the event is 8000 ZMW"       — budgeting, not a payment
    "the price is 350 ZMW per unit"          — pricing, not a payment
    "salary is 6500 ZMW per month"           — compensation disclosure
    "the invoice total comes to 3400 ZMW"    — invoice summary
    "total cost is 2200 ZMW including delivery"
    "we need to collect 10000 ZMW by end of month"
    "if you pay 500 ZMW now you get a discount"   — conditional, no completed verb
    "once you send 1200 ZMW we will proceed"      — conditional instruction
    "normally we charge 800 ZMW for this"         — pricing norm
    "please send the 1200 ZMW today"              — imperative instruction
    "let's split the 600 ZMW equally"             — cost-sharing proposal
    "remind me to pay the 400 ZMW tomorrow"       — reminder
    "he owes the company 5000 ZMW"                — debt statement (owe not in NEGATIVE_PHRASES for this phrasing)
    "500"                                          — bare number, score=2 (amount only at threshold)
    "3 guards reported today"                      — bare number, operationally irrelevant

  Root weakness: The engine has no requirement for a verb signal to be present.
  Amount + currency is sufficient to pass threshold=2. Messages that state a
  value without describing a completed action are indistinguishable from actual
  transactions at the scoring level.

  ROOT CAUSE 2 — Verb only, no amount, no currency, no negative context  (8 entries)
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Pattern: verb=True  amount=False  currency=False  negative=False
  Typical score: 2 (exactly at threshold)
  A single verb signal hits the threshold with no corroborating signals.

  Examples:
    "Received"                    — bare verb, no financial context
    "Paid"                        — bare verb, no financial context
    "maybe I will transfer it on Monday"   — "transfer" verb fires; "maybe" not in NEGATIVE_PHRASES
    "make sure to transfer the money before noon"  — imperative "transfer" fires; no negative guard
    "payment not received yet"    — "received" fires; negation ("not") not parsed by regex
    "the transfer is pending"     — "transfer" fires; status, not a completed action
    "money not yet received"      — "received" fires; negation not parsed
    "I never got the refund"      — "got" fires; negation not parsed

  Root weakness: The VERB_PATTERN matches the word "received", "transfer", "got"
  regardless of negation or modal context. The engine cannot distinguish "received"
  from "not received" or "transfer" from "make sure to transfer". Negation parsing
  is absent from the rule set.

  ROOT CAUSE 3 — Negative context fires but penalty is insufficient  (4 entries)
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Pattern: negative=True  score exactly = 2 (threshold)
  The -4 penalty cancels out positive signals but the total still lands at
  exactly the threshold when three positive signals are present (6-4=2).

  Examples:
    "Planning to transfer 5000 ZMW next week"  — verb(+2) + amt(+2) + curr(+2) - neg(-4) = 2
    "Maybe transfer the 300 ZMW later"          — same signal profile, score=2
    "have you received the 1000 ZMW?"           — verb(+2) + amt(+2) + curr(+2) - neg(-4) = 2
    "did you get the 300 ZMW I sent?"           — verb(+2) + amt(+2) + curr(+2) - neg(-4) = 2

  Root weakness: When all three positive signals fire simultaneously with the
  negative context flag, the net score is exactly 2 — the threshold. The negative
  penalty of -4 was designed to cancel two positive signals (4-4=0) but cannot
  block messages where all three positive signals accumulate to 6.

  ROOT CAUSE 4 — Verb + amount + currency present, contextual guard missing  (4 entries)
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Pattern: verb=True  amount=True  currency=True  negative=False  score=4 or 6
  These are the most dangerous false positives: they score identically to real
  transactions and cannot be distinguished by the current rule set.

  Examples:
    "Sent ZMW yesterday"                     — score=4  (verb+currency, no amount detected)
    "I heard you received 5000 ZMW"          — score=6  (verb+amt+curr, reported speech)
    "they said he paid 1000 ZMW already"     — score=6  (verb+amt+curr, third-party report)
    "apparently 2000 ZMW was transferred"    — score=6  (verb+amt+curr, hearsay passive)

  Root weakness: The engine has no concept of grammatical subject or attribution.
  "received" in "I heard you received" is treated identically to "received" in
  "Received 500 ZMW from James". Reported speech cannot be detected with the
  current regex-only approach.

SECTION 5 — THRESHOLD RECOMMENDATION AND RULE IMPROVEMENTS (STEP 9)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  RECOMMENDED THRESHOLD: Keep threshold=2, apply targeted rule additions.

  Rationale:
    Moving to threshold=3 reduces FP count from 34 to 20 but introduces 4 false
    negatives. Two of those FNs involve K-notation ("K500", "500K") — a core
    Zambian currency convention that real users will use frequently. Missing those
    in production is a harder failure than 14 extra Gemini calls. The correct fix
    is not a higher threshold but smarter rules that block specific FP patterns
    without raising the threshold globally.

  RECOMMENDED RULE ADDITIONS (in priority order):

  1. Require verb OR (amount AND currency) — not either alone
     ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     Current: threshold is a simple sum. amount+currency=4 passes without any verb.
     Proposed: add a hard gate — if transaction_verb_detected=False AND the message
               does not contain both amount AND currency, score should not clear threshold.
     Impact: eliminates "500", "3 guards reported today", "Received", "Paid" as FPs.
     Affected FP categories: ROOT CAUSE 1 (partial), ROOT CAUSE 2 (partial).

  2. Extend NEGATIVE_PHRASES to cover imperative instructions
     ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     Missing phrases (currently not in NEGATIVE_PHRASES):
       "please send", "make sure", "remind me", "let's", "once you", "if you",
       "normally", "I heard", "they said", "apparently", "maybe I"
     Adding these to NEGATIVE_PHRASES would add -4 to the FP entries in ROOT CAUSE 2,
     ROOT CAUSE 3 (partial), and ROOT CAUSE 4.
     Estimated FP reduction: ~14 entries across root causes 2 and 4.

  3. Strengthen the negative context penalty from -4 to -6
     ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     Current: verb(+2) + amount(+2) + currency(+2) - negative(-4) = 2 (passes threshold).
     Proposed: negative_context weight = -6.
     Effect: verb+amt+curr-neg = 6-6 = 0 (blocked). No change for partial-signal messages.
     Impact: eliminates the 4 ROOT CAUSE 3 FPs entirely.
     Risk: no current TPs have negative_context=True, so this change carries zero FN risk.

  4. Add negation awareness to the verb pattern
     ─────────────────────────────────────────────
     Current: VERB_PATTERN matches "received" regardless of context.
     Proposed: detect "not received", "never got", "not yet received" as a negated verb
               (i.e. add "not received", "never got", "not yet" to NEGATIVE_PHRASES).
     Impact: eliminates "payment not received yet", "money not yet received",
             "I never got the refund" from FPs.

  5. Add contextual attribution phrases (reported speech)
     ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
     Current: no guard against "I heard X", "they said X", "apparently X".
     Proposed: add "I heard", "they said", "apparently", "I was told" to NEGATIVE_PHRASES.
     Impact: eliminates 3 ROOT CAUSE 4 FPs (the hearsay score=6 entries that are
             currently indistinguishable from real transactions).

  PROJECTED IMPACT OF ALL FIVE CHANGES AT THRESHOLD=2:

    Current  : TP=59  FN=0  TN=47  FP=34  Acc=0.757  F1=0.776
    Projected: TP=59  FN=0  TN≈67  FP≈14  Acc≈0.900  F1≈0.894

    The 14 remaining FPs after these changes are the conditionals and pricing
    statements where amount+currency are present and no NEGATIVE_PHRASE fires —
    these require semantic understanding beyond regex capability and represent
    an irreducible noise floor for a rule-based approach.

SECTION 7 — SUMMARY OF FINDINGS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  1. The scoring engine achieves perfect recall (1.0) at threshold=2.
     No real transaction is missed. This is the primary requirement for a
     financial recording system and the engine meets it fully.

  2. The false positive rate (42%) is high but manageable. FPs represent
     unnecessary Gemini calls, not missed transactions. The AI extraction
     layer downstream will reject these — they will produce low-confidence
     or null extraction results and not be persisted as Transactions rows.

  3. The threshold cannot be raised without accepting transaction misses.
     Moving to threshold=3 introduces 4 FNs including two K-notation messages
     that represent common Zambian user input. This is not acceptable.

  4. Five targeted rule additions can reduce FPs from 34 to approximately 14
     without any change to the threshold and with zero FN risk. These changes
     should be implemented and re-evaluated before any production deployment.

  5. A permanent irreducible FP floor of ~10-14 entries exists for
     a regex-based classifier. Entries like "I heard you received 5000 ZMW"
     require semantic or syntactic parsing (subject detection, attribution,
     negation scope) that is beyond the rule set's capability. These will
     always require AI filtering downstream.

  6. The scoring weight for negative_context should be increased from -4 to -6
     to prevent three-signal messages from bypassing the negative guard entirely.
     This is a zero-risk change with no FN impact.
